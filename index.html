Gel Pisi Pisy

Short educational videos about cats and other surprising animals, in Turkish.

Gel Pisi Pisy makes one-minute vertical videos: each tells the real, sourced story of one animal. The videos are published on our own accounts on Instagram (@gelpisipisy), YouTube and TikTok.

To publish, we use a small desktop tool that runs only on our own computer. It signs in to our own accounts through the platforms' official APIs (TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, Instagram API) and uploads videos we made ourselves. It is not offered to other users.

Images, clips and narration are made with AI tools and are labeled as AI-generated on every platform.
